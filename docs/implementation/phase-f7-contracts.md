# Phase F7: Contracts

**English** | [繁體中文](phase-f7-contracts.zh-TW.md) | [简体中文](phase-f7-contracts.zh-CN.md)


`PublishContract` will write the client, version, base PriceVersion, fixed and seat fees allowed, valid intervals, Net30 and optional follow-up prices as unchanged ContractVersion. The underlying price still provides currency differences and volume components; The contract only covers the fixed and seat prices. Contract offer and acceptance have independent inputs and fingerprints, and normal self-acceptance commands cannot misuse contract offer.

Acme contracts are fixed at $40, $7 per seat, five seats, $75, `invoice_contracts` marked price. The service is open immediately upon the approval of the contract and invoice, entitlement justified as `enterprise_contract_net30`; It's a fictitious fact that no payment is successful. Payment operations are permanent but not immediately sent to the provider. When `CollectDueContractInvoices` expires, it is the original operation to create an outbox, with no capture added. Contract receipts can be processed after the end of the service period, as Net30 receipts should not disappear with the service period.

During the term of the contract, the invoices will be issued at the same overwriting price as the new Net30 invoice. If the contract expires without a follow-up price, keep `contract_next_price_missing` hold, stop the automatic renewal price, and change the entitlement projection to suspended; In the future, the collection of old receipts will not be free to choose the current price of the catalog. `contract_transitions` prevents repeated switching if the contract specifies in advance a subsequent price that has been published and is valid at the expiration date, and switches the deadline to a new assignment and subscription revision; Subsequent renewal at the general self-support price. The following service period is only partially covered by the contract, but automatically stops voting when the contract expires, requiring a manually defined segmentation policy.

CLI "Entrepreneurial Contracts with Net30" provides publication, offer, acceptance and expiration receipts; State search for source of contract for the demo version and subscription. Verification covers $75, unpaid opening, no capture before the Net30, due date operations, receipts, lack of follow-up price holds, and explicit follow-up price conversion only once within the deadline.
